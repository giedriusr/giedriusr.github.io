01About

I am a software engineer who ended up caring more about the business than the code. I started shipping Ruby on Rails applications in 2010, founded a product of my own, grew into leading an engineering team at one of the largest retailers in the Baltics, and now work as a solutions architect for companies that need their internal processes to just work.

The work is usually unglamorous and valuable: order flows, warehouses, invoicing, approvals, reporting, the systems people use all day without thinking about them. I like making those systems boring, reliable and fast, and I like teaching them new tricks with AI agents where it actually saves someone an afternoon.

When I am not working, I am usually somewhere near a rally stage or a gravel road.
